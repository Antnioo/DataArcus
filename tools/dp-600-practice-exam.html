Questions

Are these real DP-600 exam questions?

No. Every question is original, written for DataArcus from the public skills outline and checked against Microsoft Learn. Real exam questions are confidential, and using leaked "dumps" breaks the exam agreement and can cost you the certification.

How close is the mock exam to the real one?

It follows the same weights, the same question styles (single answer, multiple answers, Yes/No statements, ordering and a case study), 100 minutes and a 700 pass mark. Your score here is an estimate, not a prediction.

What changed in the October 2026 update?

The exam now puts more weight on preparing data (45 to 50%) and covers newer topics such as OneLake catalog, Real-Time hub, Direct Lake on OneLake and OneLake security. Read the official study guide for the full list.

What else should I use to prepare?

Hands-on time in a Fabric trial workspace, the free Microsoft practice assessment, and the Learn pages linked under every explanation here.
