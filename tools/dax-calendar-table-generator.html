Questions

            
How do I add this table in Power BI?

            
In Power BI Desktop go to Modeling → New table, paste the DAX and press Enter. Then right-click the table, choose Mark as date table, and pick the Date column. Relate Date to the date column of your fact table.

            
How does it get Hijri dates without Power Query?

            
Power BI has no Hijri functions. The generator calculates every Hijri month start in your range and embeds them in a small DATATABLE inside the DAX. Each date then looks up its Hijri month, so the table works offline and refreshes instantly.

            
Are the Ramadan and Eid dates exact?

            
They follow the official Saudi Umm al-Qura calendar. Countries that rely on local moon sighting can differ by one day. If yours did, edit that month's start date in the DATATABLE.

            
How do I compare this Ramadan with last Ramadan?

            
Put Ramadan Day on the axis and Hijri Year in the legend. Every Ramadan lines up day by day, even though the Gregorian dates move about 11 days earlier each year.

            
Why relative offsets?

            
Filter on Month Offset between -2 and 0 to always show the last three months. It updates by itself every day, so nobody has to change a slicer again.
