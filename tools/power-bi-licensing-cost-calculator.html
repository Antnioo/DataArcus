Questions

Do report viewers need a Power BI license?

Yes, unless the report sits on a Fabric capacity of F64 or larger. Below F64, everyone who opens a shared report needs Pro (or PPU for PPU workspaces). On F64 and above, viewers only need the free license. Creators always need Pro.

When is Premium Per User worth it?

For small teams that need Premium features like paginated reports, bigger models or more frequent refreshes, but have too few viewers to justify F64. Remember that everyone reading PPU content needs PPU too.

Reservation or pay-as-you-go?

A 1-year reservation is about 40% cheaper if the capacity runs all month. Pay-as-you-go suits testing or capacities you pause outside working hours.

Are these prices exact for the UAE or Saudi Arabia?

They are US list prices converted at the fixed dirham and riyal pegs. Local Microsoft partners and regional data centers can price differently, so confirm with your reseller before you budget.
