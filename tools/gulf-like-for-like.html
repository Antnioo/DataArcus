    Questions

    Why is March not like last March?

    Ramadan starts about 11 days earlier each Gregorian year, and the two Eids move with it. In 2026, March holds 19 Ramadan days and 3 Eid al-Fitr days; March 2025 held 29 and 2. A year-over-year line on those two months compares different seasons.

    Where do the dates come from?

    From the dates announced in the UAE (Oman's own where they differ), the same table DataArcus's DAX calendar generator writes. Dates not announced yet are estimates from the Umm al-Qura calendar and are marked as estimates.

    What does it not know?

    Your data. It never sees a number of yours, so it cannot say whether your business moves with Ramadan; it says when the calendar under two months differs. Years outside the dates DataArcus keeps are answered "not known".
