Is your Power BI report ready for Arabic? The checklist

19 kinds in six groups, on one printable page, in English and Arabic.

Most Power BI reports in the Gulf are built in English first, or by someone who reads from the left. Then a manager asks for the Arabic version. Every place, order and alignment on the page stays as it was built: for a reader who starts at the left.

The checklist takes the 19 kinds from our article Is Your Power BI Report Ready for Arabic? and puts them on one page, each with its fix. Each kind has one of three answers:

  • DataArcus does it, through your approval. Nothing is written before you approve the plan.
  • A Power BI Desktop setting. On an Arabic page you built yourself: the setting to change, by name.
  • Power BI has no setting for it. Said plainly, with what you can do instead.

The six groups: where the eye starts, text, charts, tables and the matrix, month and day names, and slicers. At the bottom: how to run the check on your report with check_report's Arabic lens. It reads the report's files on your computer, never your data, and changes nothing.
