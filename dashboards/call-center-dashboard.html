Showcase: E-commerce Customer Service Analysis

A dynamic report designed to turn complex customer service data into a clear roadmap for improving your e-commerce support operations.

The Challenge: Scaling E-commerce Support

As an e-commerce business grows, so does the volume of support tickets for order tracking, returns, and product questions. This showcase demonstrates how we build a command center for managers to monitor performance, reduce resolution times, and boost customer satisfaction.

Our Solution: A Unified Support Dashboard

This report is a practical example of how Power BI can transform your support data (from Zendesk, email, etc.) into actionable insights, highlighting our expertise in:

  • Support Ticket Analytics: Track key metrics like first response time, tickets solved, and resolution rate by agent and channel.
  • Data Modeling: Integrate support tickets with sales data (e.g., from Shopify) to see how service quality impacts customer lifetime value.
  • Root Cause Analysis: Identify the most common reasons for customer contact (e.g., 'Where is my order?') to proactively improve your processes.
  • Executive Interface Design: A simple, clear dashboard that lets managers quickly see the health of their support operations.

Key Features & Technologies Used:

Power BI DAX Customer Service Analytics Data Modeling Pattern Analysis

The dashboard is fully interactive. You can use filters to drill down by support teams, call types, or time periods.

Dashboard Capabilities for E-commerce Support

This showcase demonstrates how a custom dashboard helps e-commerce support managers make faster, data-driven decisions:

  • Real-time Operations View: Monitor average response times, ticket volume, and agent availability to manage daily workflow effectively.
  • Agent & Team Performance: Analyze performance to identify top performers and pinpoint who needs additional training on specific product or policy questions.
  • Peak Hour Analysis: Discover the busiest days and hours for support inquiries to optimize staffing and reduce customer wait times.
  • Improving Customer Satisfaction: Link satisfaction scores (CSAT) to resolution times and ticket types to identify the biggest opportunities for improving the customer experience.
