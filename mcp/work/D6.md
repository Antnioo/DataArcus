### Decisions I made
1. The written colour's own contrast is computed on the approval page (lib/approval-window.mjs, with the design engine's
   `contrast`), not added to `assets/js/report-a11y.js`: the a11y result already holds both colours and the card; a
   change there would mean a ?v= bump chain on the Theme Generator in a wave where other builders touch the same line.
2. The refusal names "the model or its report" only for a theme plan (its print covers the report's theme, R6 F07);
   create_report's and the scripts' plans pin the model alone, so their sentence is unchanged (and true).
3. The theme's source is said as `from the theme of report "<name>" (<theme file>)`, `... and the colours on its visuals`,
   or `from the colours on the visuals of report "<name>"`, by the source generate_theme reports.
4. The twin's month script is its own group, "Arabic month script" (not "Format script", which is create_report's);
   the theme file is "Theme file". The twin's file list is computed by `twinOutputs` beside `writeTwin`, by its rules.
5. The Arabic sentence counts names given in displayNames that hold Arabic letters, beside the fixed list's proposals;
   with neither: "no field has an Arabic name, from the fixed list or in displayNames".
