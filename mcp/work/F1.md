### L2: the pills' cause (evidence) and the variants
No evidence names the cause. What the evidence says:
- Microsoft's capabilities (`@microsoft/powerbi-core-visual-schema`, the version mcp/package.json pins): the button
  slicer's item text is the "Values" card, `value`; `label` is the "Highlight label" role's text.
- Microsoft's instance selectors (same package): state selectors (default, hover, press, selected, mixed) are listed for
  fill, outline, text, accentBar, background, label, padding, spacing, selectionIcon, expansionIcon: NOT for `value` or
  `fillCustom`, the names the capabilities use.
- Microsoft Learn ("Create and use a button slicer"): the format cards by name only (Callout values, Label); no file shape.
- Microsoft's validator: takes both shapes (with and without the state selectors).
- No visual.json Desktop saved holds a button slicer (the proof's out/, U1's research: list/dropdown slicers only).
- The proof's screenshot: the tiles drew Power BI's own grey, not our calm accent fill: our `default`-keyed entries
  were not used, or not all.
- The one measured rule for this family (round 0, the new card, Desktop 2.158): `value`, `label` and `outline` are used
  only under the `default` selector; `fillCustom.show` only without one.
Then the owner's idea (reviewer, 14:16), with the proof's own screenshot as evidence: the pill tiles draw SHORTER
than the dropdown box below them and the pills' headers BIGGER than the dropdown's. The button slicer has no header
card of its own (Microsoft's capabilities list none); its header is the container title, which we wrote with no size,
so Power BI's larger title took the height and left tiles too low for the text, which a button slicer hides rather than
cuts. This is the leading cause and the shipped default now (V0, commit `c0527a9`):
- the header (container title) at the tiles' text size, in the report's font;
- padding 4 on the tiles (default state) and the value and outline under `default` only (V1's change);
- the height: `pillH` (pbip-sizing.js, next to `slicerH` = 16 + 4 pt, measured in Desktop 2.157: 48 at 8pt, 76 at
  15pt): the dropdown's title share ceil(8 + 2 pt) + one line of tiles ceil(1.6 pt) + 2 x 4 padding + 4 = 49 at 8pt,
  56 at 10pt, 74 at 15pt. A slot the writer gives a slicer is at least slicerH high (16 + 4 pt): from 10pt up that holds
  the pills at the slicer text size (10pt: 56 vs 56; 15pt: 76 vs 74); where a slot is lower the text goes down a size at
  a time to 8, and a slot too low even at 8 keeps the dropdown, said with why in the answer's slicerStyle list. On the
  smallest pages (slicer text 8-9pt) a slot of exactly slicerH height (48-52) is 1 short, so those pills become a
  dropdown (Seen, not in scope: the slot heights are not changed here).
