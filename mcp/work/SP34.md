## Decisions I made
- The characterisation is a website suite in a browser (the page is the only caller; no test imported the file),
  hashing every step, so no bank text is stored or printed; the clock is fixed with `page.clock.setFixedTime` (timers
  run normally) and moved to drive the timer; Math.random is reseeded before every action, so other scripts' draws
  never shift the exam's.
- The draw of a mock (`pickMock`, `pickRenewal`) lives in exam-session.js with the machine, taking the random source and
  `rank` (the progress's order: unseen, wrong, right) as inputs; the same calls to the random source in the same order.
- The progress's adapter for the mock (`mockStorage`) is the one place the mock is saved; a submitted mock saves the
  progress (recordMock) and then the cleared mock (end): two writes where there was one, the same text in the end.
- The study plan's status is a key (new, weak, mid, good) from exam-progress.js; its words stay in the view.
- The keyboard, the reset button, the language observer and the deep links are the page's DOM: in exam-view.js.
- The outline file's weights are applied in dp600-sim.js (settings); the note is drawn by the view (`outline(data)`).
- New stamps: exam-grade, exam-session, exam-progress `20261104sp34`; exam-view and dp600-sim `20261104sp34d`, on the
  4 exam pages, each module before dp600-sim.
