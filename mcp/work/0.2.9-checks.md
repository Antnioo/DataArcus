## The decision needed
The working report's 4 **warnings** (`PBIR_FILTER_NAME_DUPLICATE_GLOBAL` x3, `PBIR_SCATTER_NO_GROUPING` x1) are classed
`canBreak` now, because the brief named the 10 errors only. The report opens with them, so the first could be
`desktopOpens`; the second says the scatter "will render a single aggregated point", which is what Desktop draws, so
`canBreak` (it may show wrong) fits it. Recommendation: move `PBIR_FILTER_NAME_DUPLICATE_GLOBAL` to `desktopOpens`, keep
the scatter as it is. One line in `DESKTOP_OPENS` (`mcp/lib/check-report.mjs`) and one check.
