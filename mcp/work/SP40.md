  the brief keeps "one file only". No left-to-right type list in twin-measures.mjs (M2): rowLabelTable's tableEx is
  the base's own test of a table, not the policy's list.
- sp40 covers sp26's 23 runs plus 2 (25, more than the brief's 10 to 15: the brief also asks for every earlier split's
  fixture). sp26's GEO project moved to twin-runs.mjs (one place, D-1016-01).
