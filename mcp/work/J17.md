## Decisions I made
- Questions apply only where they matter: the date role when a fact table of the plan has two or more date columns
  (or relationships to the calendar); the grain and the scope only on a seasonal page (a Ramadan, Eid or Hijri page
  filter or axis, or a Hijri / season-day grain asked for). A plain Gregorian plan with one date asks nothing.
- What blocks the plan id: a confirmed role nothing serves (inactive relationship and not every plan measure uses
  USERELATIONSHIP on it; no relationship at all), a role that is not a candidate, "all" with a Hijri-year filter, a
  year the page doesn't keep (the exact filter given), a grain whose axis is another one (the column to use given), a
  grain or year the calendar has no column for (the add_gulf_calendar offer). An unanswered question does not block:
  it comes first in sayFirst and the approval page says "not answered" (see Decision needed).
- The role served = the trend's axis is the role column itself, or its relationship to the calendar is active, or
  EVERY plan measure uses USERELATIONSHIP with that column (one that doesn't would follow the other date). The
  model's measures that do are named in the refusal; DataArcus never writes one.
- Calendar fields: declared only for the Calendar Generator's own table (its comment) with its exact column names;
  otherwise found by name = candidate; the Hijri-year column must be confirmed (hijriYearColumn) before a one-year scope
  is wired. The year's filter is not added for the person: the plan names the exact filter to pass in pageFilters,
  so plan_layout and create_report get the same pinned inputs (no change to the writer or the gate).
- Status: the generator's calendar with Is Estimated Date: a year with an estimate in gulf-dates.js (1448 on) is
  "estimated", earlier "announced" (the UAE's, they match Saudi Arabia's); "every Ramadan" = mixed, said with the
  first estimated Hijri year; any other calendar "cantTell". Another country than the UAE / KSA adds its limit
  (Umm al-Qura, it may announce another day). Shown in the plan and on the approval page, not written in the report.
- Memory: the person's answers, for the session only (like J18's terms), confirm-memory.js date records: the field
  answers hashed. Recorded when the person gives them (at plan_layout), not after the write. Evidence: the date role's
  candidates (type, sort-by, the relationships from each with its active flag); the seasonal answers' calendar columns
  (type, sort-by). A flipped relationship drops the role only; a changed calendar column drops the seasonal answers.
- Weekends: the offer names the person's country's own weekend option (the generator's), never a default country, and
  says the generator has no option for an emirate's own government weekend (Sharjah) or the Friday half day.
