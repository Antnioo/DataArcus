## CHANGELOG line
- J17 (B-170, the owner, 20 Oct: "continue the held jobs"): Gulf business dates and seasonal scope are asked, never
  guessed. With the model, plan_layout lists the structural date candidates (each date column of the plan's fact
  tables, its relationship to the calendar and whether it is active: an active relationship is not business intent)
  and asks, one question at a time in English and Arabic (gulfDates.askNext): which date (order date, ship date or
  another named one), which grain (Gregorian, Hijri, season day) and, for a seasonal page (a Ramadan, Eid or Hijri
  filter or axis), one Hijri year or every season (the year only from the person). A role no active relationship or
  plan measure (USERELATIONSHIP) serves stops the plan with why; a seasonal page with an unanswered question gets no
  plan id; a missing calendar column offers add_gulf_calendar with the person's country and years. Estimated versus
  announced dates and the country's limits are carried into the plan and the approval page. Answers are remembered
  for the session per model and dropped when a relationship or column changes. Nothing edits a relationship and
  nothing written holds USERELATIONSHIP.

## What was found first (865f6bd)
- create_report already notes (D14) that a page filter `Is Ramadan = true` keeps every Ramadan and names a Hijri-year
  column found by name; the year comes from the person. plan_layout says nothing before the plan: a "Ramadan only"
  plan gets a plan id with no scope asked.
- plan_layout's time axis (`Bind.suggest`'s date, or planFields.timeAxis) follows the active relationship silently; a
  fact table with an Order Date and a Ship Date (one inactive relationship) is never asked about.
- gulf-health.js reads a calendar's columns by name (candidates) and the generator's own calendar from its DAX
  (declared); its findings carry the estimated/announced notes (GC_ESTIMATES, GC_DATES_NOTE). gulf-dates.js holds
  the announced dates (checked to 2026-05-27; 1448 on are estimates).
- The calendar generator's weekend options: the six countries' rules, sat-sun, fri-sat, fri, sun. It has no Sharjah
  and no Friday half-day option.
- confirm-memory.js keeps health answers (hashes) and J18's term records; no record kind for a date answer.
