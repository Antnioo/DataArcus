## Plan

own last line, nothing re-typed. A small mover (kept out of the repo: an acorn walk of the file) lists every top-level

## Decisions I made
1. **The mover, not hand edits.** Every statement moved verbatim by a script (acorn: names declared and read per
   top-level statement); imports and exports computed from those names, so nothing is mistyped and no unused import is
   added. Kept out of the repo (a one-off; the commits are the record).
2. **Shared helpers to new lib modules, not to answer.mjs / plans.mjs / files.mjs:** those are imported by the lib
   itself (arabic-twin, check-report...); moving the server's helpers in would make them import the model reader, zod
   schemas and the tools' limits, and risk cycles. Named by what they do (tool-answer, tool-inputs, tool-limits,
   plan-keys, fix-scripts, hidden-names, report-fields, report-inputs, report-said, report-run): no "utils".
3. **A helper only one tool uses stays with that tool** (e.g. exactArtifact and the sample and redesign plans with
   plan_layout), as the brief says, though it keeps plan_layout.mjs at 77 KB.
4. **ctx = { LIMITS }** only: VERSION and the instructions are the entry's alone; nothing else is per-server.
5. **Tests: one helper, same counts.** The claims test keeps one "./server.mjs" entry reading the joined text (adding
   each file would have raised its count); every other source read reads the joined text in place of the one file.
6. **Tool modules named by the tool** (`tools/read_model.mjs`, underscores as the tool's name), registered under
   `registerReadModel`-style names in server.mjs.
7. **MONTHS_AR kept** as it stood (a dead constant a test reads), not removed: no change here.
8. **The twin's copy before its pages** in the commits and in TWIN_STEPS (pages imports the copy's langButton).
9. **WORK.md not touched** (G1's file, the brief): this file is S9's memory; the reviewer adds the WORK.md line.
