## Reuse

**Picker: partly.** `suggest`/`build` are pure and the website uses them as a black box. Missing: the server-only rules (A3-2/A3-3), so a third product gets a weaker binding than the MCP; a documented shape (A3-9); file I/O and DOM (`fromFolder`, `fromFile`, `renderPicker`) share the module (A1-8).

**Writer: partly.** `build(o)` is I/O-free (files plus notes) and both products use it. Missing: a contract for its ~30 option keys, `texts` and field properties; it depends on the engine's slot shapes and `REPORT_TEXTS`; it moves slots (A2-3); a caller must know the server's post-processing to match the MCP.

## Bias

- Name word lists drive every choice: `MAIN`, `COUNTISH`, `CAT` (includes "emirate"), `VARIANT`, `STALE`, `NOT_CAT*`, `DATE_TABLE`, `NUMBERISH`, `DATEISH`, `DATE_PARTS`, `NAME_LIKE`, `SORT_BY`, `ID`, `NOT_SUM`, `SHORT_MONTH` (pbip-bind.js:180-224, 367, 420-421), and in the writer `TIME_WORDS`, `NAMES`, `Bind_nameLike`. English plus Arabic only (A3-1, A3-5, A3-6, A3-8).
- Gulf-calendar exact names in server.mjs (`Day Name`, `Day Short`, `Day of Week`, `… (Arabic)`, `Ramadan Day`), from golden tasks 3-4 (A3-2).
- English text assumptions: `asBar` measures English day and month names (A3-7); `counts` makes English names unless the caller passes `names` (pbip-bind.js:427).
- Magic sizes: row pitch 1.415, tooltip 22/row + 46, axis room 40%, Tahoma ×1.06, phone 323, dropdown 76 are in DESKTOP-TESTS.md. Not recorded: `columnRoom` +10 (code: "not measured yet"), `SLOT_GAP = 40` (A2-3), `asBar`'s `widest + 6`, `railFit`'s pad 10/gap 8, 560 (G8).
- Golden numbers: 843c8dc's moves (`longTitleUses` 81→86, `suggestChars` 5610→5111) carry a "why". No portfolio-report tuning in these files on main (G1 is on the branch).

## Seen, not in scope

- `connection()` (pbip-bind.js:516-519): workspace name not URL-encoded (quotes doubled, so safe).
- The picker's `langNote` and `twinsOf` keys `T[c]` (pbip-bind.js:292, 527-528) and the server's `keyOf` are ambiguous for names that contain `]`.
- `named` (server.mjs:782) mutates shared field objects (KPI, `measure`, `tip` renamed together; undocumented).
- Already known and not repeated here: B-03, B-04, B-05, B-06 (`columnRoom`'s 12 letters), B-14/T1 (`mirrorChart` writes `invertAxis`, pbip-export.js:275), B-20, B-22, B-23 (`grid.imageHeight`), A1-11 (`PCT` word list), A2-3 (`absorb`/`dropLines`/`grown`/`railFit`).
