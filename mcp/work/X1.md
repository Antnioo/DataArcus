## Decisions I made
1. The option is `into: "separate" | "same"` (no zod default, so a planId call can be checked against the plan's own
   option: a mismatch is refused, "Nothing was written"), and `tabsShown: "en" | "ar"` (default "en").
2. The Arabic pages get NEW page ids, visual names, filter names, bookmark names and tooltip page bindings (20 hex digits
   of a hash of the original's: the same at the plan and the write, never one the report has), as Desktop's own
   "Duplicate page" does; every Arabic link (page tabs, tooltip pages, drillthrough, bookmarks, interactions) is remapped.
3. The empty state's measure is not rewritten in place (the English pages share the file): a new "No data AR: ..."
   measure, and the Arabic visuals moved to it (field, queryRef, selectors, nativeQueryRef). The original's title
   measures stay used by the English pages, so `notShown` is empty in this mode.
4. The language button takes the look of the page's own non-current page tab (colour, hover, font, size, height) and
   sits in the tabs' group; a report with no page tabs gets a plain outlined button at the top of the page. Width = the
   larger of 0.45 em a character and check_report's letter-by-letter page-button measure. Free place = scanned from the
   end, never overlapping a visual except one holding it whole (the header band). Not placed: said in the plan, why.
5. Phone: in the tabs' row, `phoneTabWidth` wide, from the same end; a page with no phone tabs gets no phone copy (said).
6. No button on tooltip and drillthrough pages (reached by their visual, not the tabs).
7. Sync: only `slicer` visuals (the one kind proven synced in the Desktop pane); groupName "<queryRef> (EN-AR n)", both
   flags true; an English slicer already in a group: its Arabic copy joins that group.
8. Bookmarks: an Arabic copy of each, labelled as approved, appended to bookmarks.json (a group's copy keeps its name).
9. PAGE_LINK_MISSING is severity error, impact "a DataArcus rule" (not "can break": the report opens; the click does
   nothing). Its source is the PBIR format, kept out of SRC (A4-10: every SRC entry is a DESKTOP-TESTS.md section).
