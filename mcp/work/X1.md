## The decisions this follows (the owner's brief, 19 Oct)
- An option, never the default: `into: "separate"` (default, today's twin, unchanged) or `into: "same"`.
- A COPY next to the original, "<name> (EN-AR).Report" and ".pbip"; never in place; the original only read (hash).
- The Arabic pages exactly as the twin builds them (buildTwin: labels, mirror, D1 waterfall, D3 phone/empty state/alt).
- The language button: actionButton, visualLink PageNavigation, navigationSection = the counterpart's page id; each
  card's `show` without a selector, the look with `selector: default` (mcp/CLAUDE.md); one line by the measured rules
  (the larger of 0.45 em a character and the page button's letter-by-letter measure, report-rules.js); at the header's
  free end (right on English pages, left on Arabic pages), never over a visual; on the phone layout too.
- Tabs: English shown, Arabic `HiddenInViewMode` (recommended, default); `tabsShown: "ar"` the reverse; tooltip pages
  stay hidden. Said plainly what a reader in the service sees.
- Slicers synced per pair with `visual.syncGroup { groupName, fieldChanges, filterChanges }` (the visualConfiguration
  schema's VisualSyncGroup, checked in mcp/schemas; Microsoft's validator runs on the copy).
- Plan lists every page pair, button, sync group and the copy's name; the approval page shows them and every file,
  grouped and counted (D6). check_report: a page-navigation button to a page the report lacks = an error.
