## CHANGELOG line
- C1 (the owner, 21 Oct): Arabic calendar columns. One shared builder (`assets/js/arabic-calendar.js`) gives a date
  table the Arabic twins of what it has: Month Name/Month Short/Day Name/Day Short (Arabic), Quarter (Arabic) «الربع 1»,
  Year Quarter (Arabic) «2025 الربع 1» (the year isolated), Fiscal Year/Quarter (Arabic), Hijri Month Name (Arabic), and
  text labels for its flags (Is Weekend, Is Working Day, Is Ramadan, Is Eid al-Fitr/al-Adha (Arabic)), each sorted by the
  English column's own sort column (or a hidden number written with it). The Calendar Generator (site and
  add_gulf_calendar) writes them; every Arabic route (plan_layout/create_report lang "ar", create_arabic_twin, the EN-AR
  copy) offers them as a TMDL script for the user's own date table, approved with the plan, never applied by DataArcus.
  Until the script is applied the Arabic report binds the English calendar columns and says which fields show English
  data and how to switch (apply the script, then plan again); after it, the Arabic twins are bound, flags included.

## What exists (read first)

- pbip-bind.js: pairTwins (text columns only), and lang "ar" swaps a date table's month/day name for its Arabic twin
  (inAr, cal.kind month|day only: not quarters, not flags).

## Decisions I made
- The builder makes every Arabic value from the date (MONTH, WEEKDAY, QUARTER, YEAR of the date column) or from the
  English column's own value (a flag, the fiscal month number, the fiscal year less "FY"), never from data read.
- The Eid flags' false label is «أيام أخرى» ("other days"): a pill reading «لا» ("no") alone says nothing of what it
  answers, and «—» reads as a blank.
- Quarters, years and flags need no sort column: «الربع 1..4» and «⁦2025⁩ الربع 1» sort right as text; months, days and
  Hijri months sort by the English column's own sort column, else the month number, else a hidden number written with it.
- Month Short (Arabic) and Day Short (Arabic) are the full Arabic names (Arabic month and day names are short already),
  so the axis that used Month Short gets its twin.
- A flag of a type other than true/false, or untyped, is left out with why (its labels can't be told from its type).
- The twin keeps the input name arabicMonth and its answer's fields (table, column, sortBy...): the full set is in
  arabicMonth.columns; the file is "<twin> - Arabic calendar.tmdl".
- A generator table made in Arabic (lang "ar") gets no twins, as before (its names are Arabic; its Quarter "Q1" and its
  flags stay as they are: Seen, below).
