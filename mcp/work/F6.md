### The eraser: what the evidence says, and the decision
- Microsoft's capabilities (`@microsoft/powerbi-core-visual-schema` data/capabilities.json, advancedSlicerVisual):
  `supportsFilterClearButton: true`, and none of its 24 cards has a property that turns the clear button off (searched
  for clear / erase / reset / restatement: only `selection.strictSingleSelect`, which is something else).
- The container (data/vco-capabilities.json): `visualHeader` lists the hover header's buttons (filter, focus, pin,
  drill, ... `showFilterRestatementButton` among them), no clear-selections button; `title` has no icon option.
- Decision: the clear icon cannot be switched off from the file, so the header row gets the eraser's height: the
  container's `spaceBelowTitle` (vco-capabilities: "Space below title", numeric) = max(4, 21 - ceil(pt x 4/3)), where
  21 is the eraser's square measured on the laptop (FINAL-ship030.md: rows 288-308) and 4 the least for a descender.
  `spaceBelowTitleArea` stays 0.
