   from the lint's extra fields (health-project: Calendar[Year Month]), `planNames` started from that one name alone and
   pinned it without the person's displayNames: the whole report kept its English names (57 runs on F3), and a second
   plan reused them only through the memory. Fixed: a first name added there starts from the approved names.
2. **A measure shown only on a flip card was in no list** (its title, alt texts, bookmarks and faces are no field
   header): declined, it was English and listed nowhere (F1 mirror: «قلب Web Orders Average Score: الوجه»); approved, it
   was not remembered for the next plan. Fixed: the sheet has a row for each flip card's measure (where: its page's
   flip card) and for each metric switcher measure (its labels and title come from the plan's names; one without an
   Arabic name was English in the slicer: "Unused One").
3. **The twin's plan cut its texts in half** (F1, F2: 48 of 97 texts shown) on a fully optioned report, pretty-printed
   past the 40,000-character cap: the texts not shown could not be labelled. Fixed: the twin's plan is answered compact
   (capAnswer `last: ['texts']`: any other list is cut first). F1's EN-AR plan is now 37,600 of 40,000: a larger report
   will be cut again (Seen, below).
4. **The twin's metric switcher choices** (the English report's field parameter rows) were not said. Fixed: a cannot
   line ("the metric switcher's choices", why, where). Its title measure was already under cannot.
5. **The EN-AR copy's «English» button** on every Arabic page was not in notTranslated. Fixed: one row, with why
   (a language switch names the language it opens, in that language).
6. **A button's own title** (twin-project's own report: "Go to details", "Reset", "Back to overview") was neither listed
   nor labelled. Fixed in report-mirror.js: listed as a text of the visual, it takes the button's approved Arabic.
7. **K, M, B on an Arabic flip card's front** (3.43K): not said. Listed now (flipCards.keptInEnglish); changing the
   letters changes what Desktop shows: the decision below.

## Decision taken (the reviewer, 20 Oct 10:35 UTC)
- Arabic unit words on an Arabic flip card's front (and on the back when the measure has no format string): the number
  `_t` (Western digits, "#,0.00"), then `_u` = « ألف» / « مليون» / « مليار», encoded for the picture once (not through
  safeDax); no bidi mark needed (the card is dir="rtl": the number reads first, the word after it). English reports'
  DAX is byte-identical (K, M, B). keptInEnglish removed. Sweep: 205 -> 225 checks (16 new unit checks + 4 English keep
  K/M/B); on 8e0b38e+ 24 of 225 red, after 225/225; flip-cards 61, v030twin 117, twin-complete 24, language-toggle 27,
  terminology-sheet 43 unchanged. My own test fixed before green: its encoder encoded the space (the picture keeps ASCII).
- The other places checked for short units written by DataArcus into Arabic text: axis-units.js already writes Arabic
  scale words (بالآلاف...); svg-kpi-compiler.js's `k1` format writes "K" for a user-designed SVG column/card (not
  language-aware; not a wave option, not written unless the person designs it): Seen, not changed.
- The twin and EN-AR copy do not get Arabic units: their faces are the English report's picture measures (listed under
  cannot). Arabic faces there need the twin to write its own flip script: proposed, not built.
