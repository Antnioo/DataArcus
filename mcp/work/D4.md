## The problem (laptop, 0.3.0-rc.1 and rc.2, Desktop 2.159)
plan_layout with redesignFrom on a copy of the lab's "Dense Ops" proposed 8 changes; written, the "No data" cards stood
BESIDE their charts as empty panels and the charts were squeezed to 240 wide. Cause, in `mcp/lib/redesign.mjs`
`gridPage`: every top-level native visual is a row member of its own; a card on the same box as its chart shares its
height, so it joins the chart's row and takes a share of the width. (DataArcus's own writer, `assets/js/pbip-page.js`,
writes the "No data" card in the chart's box ONE LAYER BELOW the chart, whose background is off; a card laid ON TOP of
a chart is the other common pattern. Both must be one unit.)

## The plan (before any code)
1. Detect units by geometry and z-order only (`position` x, y, width, height, z of the report's own visual.json files;
   never a text, never a title: untrusted), among the page's top-level visuals (not groups, not visuals in groups):
   - v lies on c when v's box is inside c's (1 unit of tolerance on each edge), and either the boxes are the same (any
     z: the writer's card is below its chart) or v is smaller and above c in z.
   - Same box: the host is the data visual (a card, new card, multi-row card, text box, shape, image or button is the
     overlay), then the lower z, then the name (a strict order: no cycle).
   - v's host is the smallest c it lies on; chains go to the outermost (the unit's lead).
   - A text box, shape or image hosts only when at most one other non-decorative visual lies on it (a panel behind one
     chart or table is a unit; a page backdrop or a section frame behind several charts is not: they keep today's
     behaviour, the frame stays, the charts move).
2. A unit is one row member, placed by its lead's box; each overlay follows its lead: an edge on the lead's edge (1 unit)
   stays on it, otherwise the overlay keeps its size and its distance from the nearer edge, clamped inside the lead.
   It never takes a slot of its own. A unit moves only when none of its visuals is hidden, custom, named twice or without
   a visual schema, and one of them is a visual the grid moves (a unit of a text box and a card over it stays, as the
   text box would). The table rule (never smaller) and the off-page and new-overlap rules see every visual of the unit.
3. The plan: the lead's entry (changes or notChanged) carries `overlays` (each by name, kind, fields, place, from, to;
   never its text) and a `unit` sentence; an overlay has no entry of its own. A change that makes a visual narrower says
   why (`narrower`). The approval page shows the unit on one line ("... with what lies on it, moved with it: ...").
4. Copy-and-patch (#68) unchanged: the overlay's new place is patches at its own /position pointers (already in ALLOW);
   verifyCopy unchanged; everything else copied as bytes.
