## Decisions I made
- `design` and `sel` live only in the store: the designer reads `S.design` / `S.sel` and changes the selection with
  `S.select(i)`; no copy of either is kept in the designer (no second state to drift).
- Panels still change the live design in place right after `S.checkpoint()` or `S.editOnce()` (as before); three
  self-contained commands use `S.apply(fn)` (add a layer, remove a value, the card background) and the three whole-design
  swaps use `S.replace(d)`. Turning every panel edit into `apply` is the panels split's job, not this move.
- A starter's undo step is taken by `replace(t)` at the end of the handler, not at its start: nothing between the two
  changes the design (the tutorial's `blank` changes the new design t), so the step holds the same snapshot.
- The suite drags with Alt held (snapping off) and finds points from shapes' and handles' boxes, never text glyphs,
  so its numbers do not depend on the machine's fonts; a step on a disabled button is a keyboard shortcut instead.
- The store emits `restore` only on undo/redo (the designer redraws everything, which also syncs the buttons), and
  `history` only when a step is pushed: the same calls in the same order as before.

## Lessons for the reviewer (what svg-editor-pointer.js and svg-editor-panels.js should know)
- Pointer: it owns `drag`, `guides` and `zoom` and needs from the designer `toDesign`, `bboxOf`, `POS`, `r1`, `clone`,
  and the render calls; its only history call is `S.checkpoint()` on the first move past 3 px (that is the grouping:
  one step per drag). Its listeners are on `stage` (pointerdown/move/up/cancel): the cleanup the map warns about.
- Panels: every edit is `S.editOnce()` (typing) or `S.checkpoint()` then an in-place change; `newField()` comes from
  the page's focusin on #kdApp, so a panel moved elsewhere must stay inside #kdApp or call it itself.
