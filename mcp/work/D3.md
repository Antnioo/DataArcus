  writer and the twin share one copy; the writer's output is byte-identical (golden, layouts and phone suites unchanged).

  no label takes the approved Arabic of the same text, shown as `labelFrom` and pinned in the plan (as B-138's page

- The empty state's measure is rewritten in place in the twin's own reportExtensions.json (no visual rewired); the
  original's unused "Title: ..." measures stay as they are and are named in `notShown`.
- `notTranslated` has one row per text (a field and the alt text repeating it are one row), to keep the plan under the
  answer cap; the approval page lists it as "Shown in English (n)".
- Fixed words: a `phrases` list in arabic-report-words.json with the writer's own Arabic, checked against the writer
  (REPORT_TEXTS and an Arabic report's page names, "No data", tooltip and empty state); joined forms ("A · B",
  "Top {1} of {2}: X", "No data: X", "A by B") drafted only when every part has a draft.
- A row of phone tabs too wide for its row is shared in proportion and said (`phone.tabsCut`); a new row is never added
  (it would move everything under it).
