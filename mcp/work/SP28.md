## Decisions I made
- The characterisation builds the writer in process (no server): 0.5 s for 14 reports, every option reachable; the
  server paths stay gated by sp7, sp13, sp5, sp3 (the twin and health fixtures).
- Diagnostics, not a shared context: a builder returns notes as ordered `[list, entry]` pairs and counts; the assembly
  applies them right after the call, so every list keeps its old order. What a builder reads of the report's state is
  passed as `known` (read only); what it would have changed is returned: the chart colour key (colourKey), the table
  pictures (svg) and the rings without their number (rings: the assembly changes reportExtensions.json), the band count
  for the phone (bands: a page Map instead of the old module-level WeakMap), the "No data" request (noData: the
  assembly names its measure with freeExt, as names are the assembly's) and the panel behind a table (panel).
- The slot plan (SLOT_PLAN) is a Map of the page's own in write, passed to the builder as `plan` (was module-level).
- The page's categories (catPlan) are worked out once for the page before the visuals (categoryPlan), not at the first
  chart that needs them: the same pure computation, so the same result.
- One empty-panel visual (`emptyPanel`) for the header's and the rail's bands and the panel behind a table (two copies before).
- The report's measure registry (freeExt, topSet, topN, notBlank, lastMonths, byMonthStart, extMeasure) is passed in
  `measures`, as SP7 passes it to the tooltip pages: the one service a builder calls.
- The family steps take the slot builder's own input x and their step state explicitly, rather than a context each.
- New stamps: pbip-visuals-charts and -tables `20261104sp28`, pbip-visuals `20261104sp28b`, pbip-page `20261104sp28`,
  theme-generator.min.js `20261104sp28b` on its three pages. BUILDER: charts, tables, visuals, then pbip-tooltip.
