## Decisions I made
- Items with refs, not names: the builders return an ordered list; `{ draw: 'reset' }` asks the assembly for a name at
  that point (a bookmark's: the Reset's, the panel's group, the open and close bookmarks), `ref`, `nameRef`,
  `parentRef`, `ofRef` and a button's `bookmark: { ref }` point at names; the assembly calls rnd in exactly the old
  sequence. Every container is followed by z + 1000 as before; the panel's group has `zAtLeast: 900000`.
- A Reset's targets `'slicers'` means "this page's slicers so far" (the old `slicerNames.slice()`), kept by the assembly.
- slicerVisual and stackHeights stay in pbip-page.js (the slicers' visual look: pbip-visuals' job) and are passed in
  `look`; they still push their own notes (slicerStyles, titles.slicers) in the same order.
- The tab-rows note's "once per page" check stays in the assembly (it reads the report-wide list).
- pbip-navigation.js reads text-measure.js, pbip-query.js and pbip-sizing.js, all before pbip-tooltip in BUILDER.
- New stamps `20261103sp13` for pbip-navigation, pbip-page and theme-generator.min.js (three generator pages).
- T9 (single buttons right to left) puts the title at the right: hand-placed slots are not mirrored, so an Arabic page
  with the title on the left has no room for buttons (that is T7's path, no page buttons).
