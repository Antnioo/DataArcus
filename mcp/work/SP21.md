## Decisions I made
- The interface. `report-answer.mjs`: `answerOf(inv, opts)` and `READ_CAP` (= answer.mjs's ANSWER_MAX). It imports
  from read-report.mjs only the screen and the inventory's own helpers (safeName, safeText, LONG_TEXT, MB,
  REPORT_LIMITS, rolesOf, roleLabel, refOf, filterText, typeName, isDecor); MB, roleLabel, filterText and isDecor
  gained `export` (nothing else about them changed). `report-identity.mjs`: `reportTheme(inv, base)`,
  `paintedColours(inv)`, `identityOf(inv)`; it imports nothing. read-report.mjs re-exports answerOf, READ_CAP,
  reportTheme, paintedColours: no importer changed. reportIdentity(src, name) stays there as
  `identityOf(inventory(src, name))`, and existingTheme (which reports to read: disk) stays there too.
- The proof that neither file reads the disk: sp21 P3 reads both files' source and fails on any import (static,
  dynamic, require) other than './read-report.mjs' and './answer.mjs' (identity: none), so no fs, path, url, readers.
- report-answer.mjs and read-report.mjs import each other (as SP20's pair). read-report.mjs has a top-level await (the
  Microsoft catalog); neither module uses the other's bindings while loading. sp21 P4 loads each of the three first
  in a fresh node and calls answerOf and reportIdentity: all pass.
- "Never its own cut": answerOf never cuts text. It fits the answer under READ_CAP by listing fewer pages (TRUNCATED)
  or fewer visuals with a cursor (MORE), as before; the tool's answer then goes through tool-answer.mjs. Kept as is.
- The brief's "area reader" does not exist in tests.json: sp21 sits in area model, beside reader-seen and v029f;
  the two new files are added to the model area's affected map (they also match report's `report-*.mjs`).
- The suite's 14 reports: copies of the 4 fixture projects and 10 made from twin-project in a temp folder; the
  fixture holds only sha256 and lengths of the answers and the identities as JSON (colours and theme names).
  scripts/tests/fixtures/bridge-project is a legacy report (no definition folder): its recorded answer is the
  reader's refusal ("not a PBIR report"), pinned as it is. 13 PBIR reports are read.
- Two guards read read-report.mjs's source for text that moved verbatim: rc23 F3 (read_report's note) and tk1 L2
  (ANSWER_MAX). Each now reads lib/report-answer.mjs, with a comment; no expected number changed (51 and 15).
