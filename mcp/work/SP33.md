## The interfaces
- **SVGEditorPointer.attach({ stage, store, pos, r1, clone, view, on })** -> `{ guides, dragging, detach() }`.
  In: `view` is the viewport transform (`hasCanvas()`, `zoom()`, `toDesign(e)`, `bboxOf(i)`); `store` gives `design`,
  `sel`. Out: store commands `select(i)` and `checkpoint()` (once per drag, at the first move past 3 px), and render
  requests `on.picked()`, `on.canvas()`, `on.moved()`, `on.dropped()`. Its only state is the gesture and the guides.
- **SVGEditorPanels.create({ $, store, K, types, pos, L, esc, clone, r1, samples, track, toast, tour, view })** ->
  `{ renderLayers, renderAddBar, renderProps, renderValues, layerAction, valueName }`. They read the store and change
  the design only after `editOnce()` (a field visit), `checkpoint()` or `apply(fn)` (a button); render requests go
  out through `view.canvas/dax/save/all`. No state of their own.

## Decisions I made
- The panels take the value helpers only they use (evalValues, isFraction, usedBy, newId, firstValue, FMT, KINDS);
  `samples()` stays with the canvas preview and is passed in; `valueName` is returned for the DAX warnings: one copy.
- The pointer gets `r1`, `clone` and `POS` from the designer (one place each), not its own copies.
- The panels keep the designer's 2-space indentation inside their factory: re-indenting would add spaces inside the
  multi-line HTML templates and change the panels' HTML (sp33 records it).
- Panels still edit the design in place after `editOnce()`/`checkpoint()` (as before); turning each into `apply(fn)`
  would change nothing visible but is a behaviour-shaped rewrite, not a move.
- sp25's sessions, helpers and state are exported and replayed by sp33 (one place for them; sp25's own records and
  checks are untouched).
- The designer never calls `detach()` (the page lives as long as the editor); M3 proves it works for a future caller.
